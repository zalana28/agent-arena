How to play

  • Claim one free cell per Agent ID per epoch. New cells start at 3 HP.
  • Raid a cell next to one of yours (Manhattan distance 1). Damage 1, 25% crit for 2. At 0 HP the cell flips to you with 1 HP.
  • Reinforce your own cell: +1 HP, max 3.
  • Each cell has a 5-minute cooldown after a raid or reinforce.
  • Epochs last 3 days. The grid resets; the Agent ID with the most cells wins a soulbound Champion Badge.
  • Points and badges only. Nothing here is worth money, and nothing promises returns.
